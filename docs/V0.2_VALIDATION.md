A Chromium-based QA pass rendered the app at the target 1194×834 iPad landscape viewport, plus a 1024×768 responsive smoke test. **102 checks passed and 0 failed.**

- Six-player TOI layout and concise right rail.
- Start, Pause/Resume, Restart, `skip 30 sec`, and `skip 5 min` controls.
- AI Insights compact state, expanded composition, four related miniatures, and all four full-screen related views.
- Player Insights default layout, every expanded widget, zero-faceoff context, four-widget repack, single-widget repack, final-widget protection, and light theme.
- Featured Insights default composition, first-stage reflow, second-stage full-screen Head-to-Head Faceoffs view, and light theme.
- Stats default composition and full-width reflow for Cumulative Points and Season Trends with paired widgets moved underneath.
- Video, Notes, Calendar, and Preferences in dark and light themes.
- Onboarding step 2 and step 4, including the 4 px white selected-card stroke and a Continue button visible without scrolling.
- Horizontal overflow checks on all tested pages and both iPad viewport sizes.
